# ShopScan
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
adaptive

## Stack
Expo React Native and TypeScript, explicitly requested by the user. iPhone and Android are required.

## Users and purpose
Grocery shoppers record barcodes, names, prices and whole-number quantities during a trip, see a subtotal, resume local sessions, and share CSV files.

## Capabilities and constraints
See docs/REQUIREMENTS.md for the approved brief. No accounts, backend, external product lookup, cloud sync or Google integration. USD is the documented initial currency. Real-device scanning and sharing require device verification.

## Brand commitments
ShopScan. Simple, clean, large touch targets, primary Scan Item action, prominent estimated total. Do not over-design.

## Accessibility
Screen-reader labels, scaling text, contrast, safe areas, keyboard avoidance, and platform navigation.
